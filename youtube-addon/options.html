CRATE for YouTube

Lets CRATE find YouTube videos and album playlists for your songs without using up YouTube’s API quota.

What it does, and what to know

When CRATE asks, this reads YouTube’s own search and playlist pages one at a time, in the background: it asks for the page directly (no tab opens), with a pause of 1.5 to 3 seconds between pages. If a page can’t be read that way it opens it in a background tab it closes again (pause 9 to 15 seconds). It remembers every answer, so the same question is never asked twice, and it stops if YouTube shows a cookie question or an “unusual traffic” page, leaving that page in front of you.

It reads pages that YouTube’s terms say only the API may be used for. It uses no account and no password, but your browser’s cookies come with every page it opens, so keep it to what you need. At most 250 pages a day.
