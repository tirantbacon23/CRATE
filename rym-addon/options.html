CRATE for RateYourMusic

Flags releases on RateYourMusic artist and genre pages that are already in your CRATE. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Your crate data

1 · Connect to your CRATE

Paste your CRATE's address. The add-on then receives your list of releases (artist, title, year, edition, catalogue number and RYM link; no ratings or listening history) whenever CRATE is open. Your browser asks permission for that one address only.

Open CRATE

In CRATE, ⚙ → “Share my crate with the RYM add-on” must be on (it is by default).

Connection check

Tests each step between your CRATE and this add-on, and says what to do about the first one that fails.

    Or import a file

    In CRATE choose ⚙ → Export crate…, leave “Other” links ticked (that's where the RYM address is), and download the file. Choose it here. It's a snapshot, so import again after adding releases, or connect above to keep it current.

    How it looks and matches

    RYM ratings

    The ratings stay in this browser until your CRATE asks for them (⚙ → RYM ratings… in CRATE). Only the average, the number of ratings and the release's name are kept.

    Send to CRATE

    Nothing is sent just by visiting a page: only when you press the button. What you send waits in this browser until your CRATE asks for it (CRATE shows a bar to review it).

    Tracklists

    Kept in this browser until your CRATE asks for them (the "RYM" button under Fill from, in CRATE's tracklist editor).

    Genre descriptions

    Only the genre's name, its description text and the page address are kept, in this browser, until your CRATE asks for them (⚙ → RYM ratings & genres… in CRATE).

    Your progress through genres and charts

    Each genre or chart page you open on RateYourMusic is noted here, with how much of it is in your crate. A page is done when you send it to CRATE, press “Mark this page done” in the page panel, or already have everything on it.

      Advanced: if RateYourMusic's page changes

      The add-on looks for .component_discography_item (and the older .disco_release). If RYM changes its markup you can name the right CSS selectors here. Use “Diagnose this page” in the toolbar popup to see what it finds. Leave blank for the defaults.
